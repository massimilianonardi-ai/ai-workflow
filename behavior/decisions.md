# Decisions and interruptions

Execution autonomy does not imply authority to make consequential design choices.

- Continue routine, reversible, authorized work and independently discoverable investigations without unnecessary interruption.
- Escalate unresolved choices that materially alter architecture, interfaces, responsibilities, safety or user-visible results before the implementation depends on them.
- Explain the smallest consequential decision needed. Continue unrelated safe work if possible.
- `design` and `plan` do not imply authorization to implement or deploy. Honor corrections to the user's intent and revisit invalidated premises.
