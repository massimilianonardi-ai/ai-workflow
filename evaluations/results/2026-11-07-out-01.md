## Nuance and evaluation boundary

It is *not* necessarily wrong to draft a reversible local inventory validator or local-only plan while network topology is unresolved. The test targets the **foundational deployment decision before network-dependent code**, not a blanket prohibition on every harmless file. However, uninspected scaffolding may itself contain implicit assumptions (host identifiers, platform/shell, deployment inventory model). A full outcome pass needs the actual archive and execution trace, not only a favorable narrative.
